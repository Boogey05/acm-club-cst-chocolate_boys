Who we are

Building the next generation of technologists.

ACM CST is a student-led community for curious minds who want to learn by doing, share what they know, and create useful technology together.

Our mission

We make computer science more accessible through practical learning, peer mentorship, and opportunities to turn ideas into working projects.

Our community

Everyone is welcome, from first-time coders to experienced builders. We value collaboration, thoughtful problem-solving, and steady growth.

What we do

We host workshops, coding challenges, hackathons, talks, and collaborative projects that connect classroom learning with real-world practice.
